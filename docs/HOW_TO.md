# How to use the consignment store app

One section per role. [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) has the full rules.

## Admins

### Before the convention

1. **Set your PIN and print your badge** on **My account**. The badge and PIN are for overrides on volunteer screens. They don't sign you in.
2. **Add staff** under **Staff**. Each person gets an email to set their password. Volunteers get a Volunteer ID, and admins get a badge. Give volunteers access to the event on their staff page.
3. **Create the event** under **Events**: the Event ID (such as `F26A`), dates, price windows, fees and selling rules. The Event ID can only change during Setup.
4. **Optional:** set reminder times on the event's status schedule. They only remind you, and statuses never change on their own.
5. **Set fee waivers** for volunteers, admins or individual sellers on **Fee waivers**. These can change until the event closes.
6. **Move to Presale** when registration opens. Fees lock when the first seller signs up.
7. **Bring in signups** on **Seller signups**: connect the Google Form and press **Sync now**, or upload the responses as CSV. Duplicates, incomplete rows and late signups go to the review list. Create the seller from there if appropriate.
8. **Test the scanners** with **Sample slips for scanner tests** on the event page.

### Check-in

1. **Move to Check-in** when sellers start arriving. Registration and listing changes stop.
2. **Overrides:** volunteers may call you over. Scan your badge on their screen, type your PIN and add a note.

### Sale

1. **Set up registers** on **Sales control**: add one per laptop. Each laptop chooses its register the first time it opens **Register**.
2. **Choose the active price window** on **Sales control**, then **move to Sale**.
3. **Change windows** when it's time. You can't while a regular cart is open. Ask volunteers to finish, or cancel stuck carts on the same page. Close the last window to end regular checkout.
4. **Watch for alerts** on your home page and **Register alerts**: two-register conflicts and Sold games scanned again. Resolve them with your badge and PIN.
5. **Admin carts** are opened on **Sales control** for purchases outside the regular windows.
6. **Sale overrides:** if Payment Received was recorded but payment failed, open the sale under **Sales** and return it to payment with your badge, PIN and a note.

### Pickup and closing

1. **Move to Pickup** once no carts are open. Volunteers return games to sellers on **Pickup**.
2. **Account for unsold games** on **Donated and abandoned scanning**: scan every unsold game still on the tables. Mark any game that can't be found as Missing from its page.
3. **Record payouts** on **Payouts**: press **Record automatic outcomes** for Nothing due and Balance forgiven, then record each Paid seller with the transaction or check number. Pay sellers outside the app. Recording freezes a seller's figures.
4. **Check the money** in **Reports**. The Revenue report says whether total sales plus balances collected equals payouts plus fees kept.
5. **Move to Post-sale** to close the event. It's refused until no games are Available, In checkout, Missing or Error, no alerts are open, and every seller who delivered games has a payout outcome. Closing can't be undone.

## Volunteers

Sign in with your Volunteer ID or email. You only see events an admin has given you.

### Check-in

1. Open the event's **Check-in** and find the seller by Seller ID or name. You can't check in your own games.
2. **Scan a slip.** Compare the screen with the slip and the game.
3. **Scan the next slip** to accept the game shown. If something doesn't match, type a reason and **Reject**.
4. **Warnings:** for an outdated or deactivated slip, check with the seller before accepting. For a game already checked in, choose **Double scanned item**, or call an admin.
5. **Finish:** press **Confirm Check-in**, check the report with the seller, then **Check-in Complete**.

### Register

1. Open **Register**. The first time on a laptop, choose which register it is.
2. **Build the sale:** scan each game. **Remove** takes a game out, and it goes to the restock table after the sale.
3. **Take payment:** press **Proceed to Payment**, enter the Sale ID in Square and take payment. Move any listed games to the restock table.
4. **Finish:** press **Payment Received**, or **Cancel Sale**, and confirm.
5. **Problems:** if a red conflict alert appears, don't take payment. Call an admin. If a game is already SOLD, set it aside, not back on the tables.

### Pickup

1. Open **Pickup** and find the seller.
2. Scan each game as it goes back to them. For donated, abandoned or never-checked-in games, ask the seller and press their answer.
3. If a game is SOLD, Error or Missing, set it aside and call an admin.

## Sellers

1. **Sign up** with the convention's form. You'll get an email to set your password, and you sign in with your Seller ID or email.
2. **Agree to the rules and fees** the first time you sign in.
3. **During Presale:**
   - **Add each game** under **My games**. You can save unfinished games and come back to them.
   - **Generate slips** once every game is complete, then print at 100% / actual size, cut them out and attach one to each game.
   - **To change a game with a slip,** deactivate it and reactivate it with changes. Its old slip stops working, so print the new one.
4. **Bring your games to check-in.** Your check-in report appears in your account afterward.
5. **After the convention:** see **Sales and fees** for what sold, your fees and your payout. It becomes your final report once the event closes, and you can sign in for 30 days after that.
